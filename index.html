Lollipops are very cool

Fun Facts:

• Modern lollipops were invented in 1908 by George Smith in Connecticut. It was namedafter his favorite racehorse, "Lolly Pop".

• The idea of lollipopsgoes back thousands of years to ancient Egypt, China, and Arabia, where people dipped fruits and nuts into honey.

• The world's largest lollipop weighed 7,003 pounds and was made by See's Candies in California in July 2012.

• The "mystery flavor" of Dum Dums isn't a random mix, it's created when the end of one batch blends directly into the start of the next batch in the vat

• Tootsie Roll produces around 16 million Tootsie Pops every single day.
